# Premium Product Feel Through Theme Depth
#learning

## Insight
After a component-library migration, the fastest way to make a UI feel premium is usually not adding more components. It is refining the shared theme, surface layering, spacing rhythm, and emphasis rules so the whole product feels intentionally designed.

## Why It Matters
Raw component migrations often improve consistency but still look transitional. Theme-level depth gives the interface a clearer visual hierarchy and makes every reused surface feel more deliberate without rewriting each screen from scratch.

## Reusable Practice
- Tune typography, border radius, shadows, and background layering at the theme level before chasing one-off polish.
- Use subtle contrast differences between shell, content, and supporting surfaces to create a stronger sense of product depth.
- Add emphasis through spacing and hierarchy first; use ornament sparingly.
- Prioritize the most visible shared surfaces because they lift the whole product fastest.
- When nested workspaces start to feel cramped, reduce duplicate framing and stack sections vertically sooner instead of forcing another responsive grid breakpoint.

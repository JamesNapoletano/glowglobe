# Plot and Structure - Orientation
#project-concept
## Purpose
Capture the higher-level narrative shape of a project, including acts, beats, arcs, subplots, and POV distribution.

## Current Reality
This area is a recommended supporting surface that complements corkboard and manuscript work, but no implementation exists yet.

## Desired Outcome
The author can see the narrative architecture of a book clearly enough to plan, revise, and balance pacing and story movement.

## Important Actors or Stakeholders
- Primary author
- Story arcs, beats, and viewpoint structures as planning abstractions

## Key Artifacts or Interfaces
- Act structure — high-level narrative phases
- Beat map — major turning points
- Subplot tracker — secondary arcs
- POV allocation view — viewpoint balance across scenes

## Constraints
- Must reinforce rather than replace the manuscript workflow
- Must align with scene-level and timeline-level planning
- Must remain flexible across different writing methods

## Open Questions
- Which structure templates, if any, should be built in?
- How formal should plot modeling be in v1?

# Lore and Glossary - Orientation
#project-concept

## Purpose
Store canon terms, history, language notes, and recurring concepts in a form that improves consistency and retrieval.

## Current Reality
This is a recommended support surface to keep reference material usable and searchable as projects grow.

## Desired Outcome
The author can quickly resolve canon questions and reuse terms consistently across scenes, notes, and planning artifacts.

## Important Actors or Stakeholders
- Primary author
- Canon concepts, groups, terms, and historical notes as reference entities

## Key Artifacts or Interfaces
- Glossary entries — term definitions and usage
- Lore notes — history, myth, canon rules
- Group-adjacent canon references that complement the dedicated `Sociums` surface

## Constraints
- Must be fast to consult during drafting
- Must support canon stability and continuity
- Must connect to manuscript and other story-bible entities

## Open Questions
- How much canon about a group should live in `Sociums` versus general lore notes?
- How much historical depth belongs in structured fields versus notes?

# Corkboard and Timeline - Orientation
#project-concept

## Purpose
Give the author visual and structured planning tools for scenes, chronology, plot flow, and pacing.

## Current Reality
The corkboard and timeline are explicitly in v1 scope, but they are not implemented yet.

## Desired Outcome
The author can arrange scenes as cards, inspect chronology, and understand plot movement without leaving the broader manuscript workflow.

## Important Actors or Stakeholders
- Primary author
- Scene and event entities as planning units

## Key Artifacts or Interfaces
- Corkboard cards — scene or beat summaries
- Timeline view — chronological ordering of events
- Plot thread markers — parallel storyline tracking

## Constraints
- Must support v1 planning needs without overpowering the manuscript-first product frame
- Must remain linked to chapters, scenes, and story entities
- Must work with local-first persistence and future sync-safe identifiers

## Open Questions
- How visual should the corkboard be in the first release?
- Should timeline support absolute dates, relative ordering, or both?

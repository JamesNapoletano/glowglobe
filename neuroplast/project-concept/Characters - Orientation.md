# Characters - Orientation
#project-concept

## Purpose
Store structured information about people and entities in the story so the author can maintain personality, history, goals, and continuity.

## Current Reality
Characters are a required product surface and part of the initial story-bible scope, but no implementation exists yet.

## Desired Outcome
The author can quickly create, inspect, and update character profiles that stay connected to scenes, relationships, arcs, and plot developments.

## Important Actors or Stakeholders
- Primary author
- Fictional characters as tracked narrative entities

## Key Artifacts or Interfaces
- Character profile — identity, appearance, voice, and traits
- Relationship graph — links to other characters or groups
- Arc notes — motivations, change over time, unresolved tensions

## Constraints
- Must support continuity-friendly structure
- Must remain easy to reference from manuscript scenes
- Must fit within a local-first, sync-ready domain model

## Open Questions
- Should relationship graphs be visual in v1 or list-first?
- Which character fields are essential versus optional?

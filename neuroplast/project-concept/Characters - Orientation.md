# Characters - Orientation
#project-concept

## Purpose
Store structured information about people and entities in the story so the author can maintain personality, history, goals, and continuity.

## Current Reality
Characters are a required product surface and part of the initial story-bible scope. The surface now supports richer wiki-style profiles and typed relationships, but derived continuity views such as automatic scene appearance summaries are still deferred.

## Desired Outcome
The author can quickly create, inspect, and update character profiles that stay connected to scenes, typed relationships, arcs, and plot developments without forcing every entry to become a full dossier.

## Important Actors or Stakeholders
- Primary author
- Fictional characters as tracked narrative entities

## Key Artifacts or Interfaces
- Character profile — lightweight core identity plus optional dossier fields for appearance, voice, history, secrets, and continuity
- Relationship directory — typed links to other characters with notes
- Arc notes — motivations, change over time, unresolved tensions

## Constraints
- Must support continuity-friendly structure
- Must remain easy to reference from manuscript scenes
- Must fit within a local-first, sync-ready domain model

## Open Questions
- Should relationship graphs be visual in v1 or remain list-first?
- Which dossier fields should later gain derived or cross-linked views?

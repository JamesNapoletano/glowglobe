# Technology - Orientation
#project-concept
## Purpose
Capture the tools, systems, inventions, and rule-based mechanisms that shape the story world.

## Current Reality
Technology is part of the requested v1 story-bible scope and may include science-fiction systems, inventions, or magic-tech hybrids.

## Desired Outcome
The author can define technologies or systems with enough structure to preserve internal logic, constraints, dependencies, and narrative use.

## Important Actors or Stakeholders
- Primary author
- Story-world systems and devices as canonical entities

## Key Artifacts or Interfaces
- Technology entry — description, purpose, rules, limitations
- Dependency map — prerequisites, components, or related systems
- Narrative usage notes — where the technology affects plot or setting

## Constraints
- Must support both hard-tech and softer rule systems
- Must be linkable to scenes, places, and factions
- Must preserve internal logic for continuity

## Open Questions
- Should technology and magic systems stay unified or split later?
- Which fields are required for v1 consistency?
